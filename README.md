# HR Breath Pacer

Calming music driven by your heart rate. It reads your pulse live from a Bluetooth smartwatch, plays generative ambient music a few beats per minute slower than your heart, and paces your breathing with a cue at the start of every inhale and exhale (4 in, 6 out by default). Your heart tends to follow the music down.
